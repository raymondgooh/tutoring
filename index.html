About me

Hi, I'm Raymond.

I'm an ASA-credentialed actuary and consultant, currently one exam away from my FSA. Day to day, I work on reserving, pricing, and IFRS 17 across Southeast Asian markets — so the material I tutor is the same material I use professionally, not something I studied once and set aside.

Since 2021, I've tutored candidates through every SOA associate exam — P, FM, FAM, ASTAM, ALTAM, SRM, and PA. I know how tempting it is to memorize a formula the night before an exam, and I know how much better it goes when you actually understand why it works. That's the difference I try to build into every session.

How we work

Two things happen in every session.

First

Understand the concept

We slow down on the material until it actually clicks — why the formula looks the way it does, not just what it says. Memorizing gets you through one question. Understanding gets you through the exam.

Then

Work through it together

We solve practice questions side by side. Watching how you approach a problem tells me exactly where you're getting stuck — and lets me share how to think about it under real exam conditions.
